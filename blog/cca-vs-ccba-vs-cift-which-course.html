CCA vs CCBA vs CIFT: Which One Fits Your Goal?

Course Comparison Updated 3 August 2026 7 min read

Three of Hartron Hisar's most popular certificates — CCA, CCBA and CIFT — sound similar and even overlap in places. But they point at different goals. Here's an honest, plain comparison so you pick the one that actually matches what you want next.

The one-line version

CCA is the long, government-recognised computer course built for office and clerk jobs — and it carries the SETC written-test exemption for the Clerk Post. CCBA is a shorter course that mixes computer basics with accounting for business-office work. CIFT pairs IT foundations with financial software like Tally and Excel for finance, aimed at accounts and banking back-office roles.

CCA — the govt-job and clerk route

Pick CCA if a Haryana government office or Clerk post is your target. It's a full 52-week course starting from computer fundamentals and going up to programming basics, and its standout benefit is the SETC written-test exemption for the Clerk Post. We explain how that exemption works in our CCA + SETC guide.

CCBA — business office and accounting basics

Pick CCBA if you want solid business computer skills with a taste of accounting in half the time. At 26 weeks it's aimed at business office assistant and operations roles — a practical choice if you don't need the full govt-clerk path but want employable office skills quickly.

CIFT — finance plus IT

Pick CIFT if your goal is accounts, banking back-office or junior financial-data work. Also 26 weeks, it combines IT foundations with financial tools like Tally and Excel for finance — closer to the money side than CCBA.

None of these is “better” than the others. The best one is simply the one that matches the job you're aiming for.

Frequently asked questions

Which is the best course among CCA, CCBA and CIFT?

There is no single best course. CCA suits government and clerk goals, CCBA suits business-office work, and CIFT suits accounts and banking back-office roles. Pick the one that matches the job you want.

Which is the shortest and cheapest?

CCBA and CIFT are both 26 weeks and cost Rs 10,000, or Rs 9,000 as a one-time lumpsum. CCA is longer at 52 weeks and costs Rs 20,000, or Rs 18,000 lumpsum.

Does only CCA give the SETC exemption?

Among these three, the SETC written-test exemption for the Clerk Post is tied to CCA. Always confirm the computer-qualification clause in the official notification for your target post.

Can a beginner take these courses?

Yes, all three start from fundamentals. CCA needs 11th pass and 12th pursuing; CCBA and CIFT need 12th pass.
