Latest Haryana Govt Jobs: Which Computer Certificate You Need

Government Jobs Updated 29 July 2026 6 min read

Haryana government recruitment moves in cycles, and a computer-proficiency requirement shows up in most clerical and office-role notifications. This guide looks at the recent 2026 HSSC notifications, explains how that computer requirement actually works, and maps it to the right Hartron course — so you're ready when the next cycle opens. We stick to what the official notifications state; always confirm details on the official HSSC website for your exact post.

What HSSC released in mid-2026

Two large recruitment drives ran through the Haryana Staff Selection Commission in mid-2026. The HSSC CET Group D notification (Advertisement No. 05/2026) was published on 18 June 2026, with online applications open from 19 June to 5 July 2026. Separately, an HSSC Group C recruitment (1238 posts) ran its online registration window in late June 2026. Both application windows have now closed — treat the dates below as reference for how the next cycle is likely to run, not as open applications.

Application windows close fast — often two to three weeks. The candidates who apply calmly are usually the ones whose qualifications were ready before the notification dropped.

How the computer requirement works

For these HSSC recruitments, the computer proficiency and typing test is qualifying in nature only — you must pass it, but the marks are not added to your merit list. In practice that means the computer test is a gate, not a scoring round: strong, steady computer skills clear it comfortably, and a recognised computer qualification is the surest way to build them. This is separate from the SETC written-test exemption path, which we cover in our SETC exemption guide.

Which Hartron course maps to the requirement

For clerk and general office posts, the CCA (Course in Computer Application) is the natural fit — a 52-week, government-recognised qualification that also carries the SETC written-test exemption for the Clerk Post. If the role leans towards accounts (junior accountant, Tally, GST), the CDA (Course in Digital Accounting) covers the same computer foundation plus practical accounting. Both are NCVET-approved and NSQF-aligned.

What to do before the next cycle

  1. Watch the source — check hssc.gov.in directly rather than relying on forwarded messages.
  2. Read the eligibility clause — note the exact computer-qualification line for your target post.
  3. Get qualified in advance — a 52-week course like CCA or CDA should be finished before you apply, not started after.
