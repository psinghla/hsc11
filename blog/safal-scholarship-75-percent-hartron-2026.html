SAFAL Scholarship: 75% Off Emerging-Tech Courses

Scholarships Updated 10 August 2026 6 min read

If a family's income has been the reason a student in Haryana held back from learning AI, cyber security or data skills, the state's SAFAL scheme is worth a serious look. It offers a 75% scholarship on emerging-technology courses at Hartron Advanced Skill Centre (HASC) for 1,000 students from Economically Weaker Sections. Applications close on 15 August 2026, so here is the honest, practical picture — what it covers, who qualifies, and how to apply.

What SAFAL actually gives you

SAFAL stands for the Government of Haryana's skill-assistance scholarship for advanced IT education. The core benefit is simple: a 75% waiver of the prescribed course fee. You pay only the remaining 25%, in two instalments. In total, 1,000 scholarships are available, reserved exclusively for students from Economically Weaker Sections (EWS).

The point of SAFAL isn't a discount — it's removing cost as the reason a capable student never gets to learn a future-facing skill.

Who is eligible

To apply, you need to meet all three conditions from the official notice:

  1. A valid PPP / Family ID (this is mandatory to apply).
  2. Annual family income below ₹1.80 lakh.
  3. Minimum qualification 10+2 (senior secondary) pass or above.

The application fee is ₹100 only (excluding GST), paid at the time you submit the form.

Merit-listed students may pick any eligible emerging-technology course at a Hartron Advanced Skill Centre, subject to seat availability. These are the courses covered under the scheme:

How selection works

Selection is merit-based, through a Computer-Based Scholarship Test held at IDDC Ambala and HMSDC Gurugram. The test is multiple-choice (MCQs) covering Basic English, Mathematics, Aptitude & Reasoning, and Computer Applications. A merit list of 1,000 students is prepared, plus a 10% waiting list; if a selected candidate doesn't take admission within two months, a waiting-list candidate gets the seat.

How to apply, step by step

  1. Keep your PPP / Family ID ready — it's mandatory and details must be correct.
  2. Apply online at the official HASC portal: hasc.hartron.org.in.
  3. Fill the form carefully and pay the ₹100 application fee — incomplete applications are rejected.
  4. Appear for the Computer-Based Scholarship Test at your allotted centre (Ambala or Gurugram).
  5. If merit-listed, choose your course and complete admission within the given window.
Last date to apply: 15 August 2026. Apply online at hasc.hartron.org.in or call the official helpline 0172-2922086. Details here follow the official SAFAL notice; always confirm on the HASC portal before applying, as the department may modify the process.
